# Side-hustle validator

A Claude skill (and a plain LLM prompt) for thinking about and launching side businesses. Its method is inspired by Chris Koerner's interview on The Diary of a CEO, "Passive Income Expert: How To Make $10k Per Month In 90 Days!".

## What it does

- **Mindset:** getting past the fear of what others think, and knowing when to quit your job and when not to.
- **Finding ideas:** where to look for inspiration and how to spot opportunities around you.
- **Picking one idea:** the two-week rule, your unfair advantage, and the path of least friction.
- **Scoring an idea:** an "unsexy score" across 9 criteria.
- **A 2-hour demand test** on Facebook Marketplace or a local equivalent, for $0–20.
- **Reverse-engineering competitors** with the Wayback Machine and SimilarWeb.
- **Launching:** first customers, sales, pricing and PR, then deciding whether to scale or kill.
- **Step-by-step playbooks:** an AI implementation agency for small businesses, niche directory sites, a local email newsletter, wedding and event rentals, vending machines, and buying a retiring owner's business with seller financing.
- **Partners and founder type:** Starter, Maintainer or Finisher.

The skill replies in whatever language the user writes in.

> This is an unofficial summary. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chris Koerner or The Diary of a CEO. The figures are his US examples, not income guarantees.

## Installation

**Claude (claude.ai and desktop):** download the `side-hustle-validator` folder and zip it on its own. Then upload the zip in Claude's settings, in the Skills section.

**Claude Code:** copy the `side-hustle-validator` folder to `~/.claude/skills/` to use it in all projects, or to `.claude/skills/` inside a single project.

**Other LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, local models):** paste the contents of `side-hustle-validator/SKILL.md` as a system prompt, custom instructions, a Custom GPT or a Gem.

## Usage

Just ask, for example:

- "I have tons of ideas and can't get started. Help me pick one."
- "Score this idea: a vending machine in an auto repair shop lobby."
- "Plan a 2-hour test for renting wedding arches in my city."
